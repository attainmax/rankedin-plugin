RankedIn career paths
No RankedIn analysis was used for this answer. These four paths are a framework for deciding what to focus on next — they are not an assessment of you. Choose between them using what you know about your own situation.

1. Job search — "I want to find a job"
   Approach: Land interviews at your target company — one company, one week, one real piece of outreach.
   First step: One company. Pick the one you would actually want to work at.
2. Business development — "I want to attract more customers"
   Approach: Turn one dream client into a real conversation. Research their problem, ship something useful, reach the person who'd actually buy — no pitching, no cold-DMs.
   First step: One company. Pick one you'd want as a client — where your effort can become visible to the people who would buy.
3. Build My Reputation — "I want to build my reputation"
   Approach: Build visible proof of what you know and how you think.
   First step: One company. Pick one whose problems you'd want to be known for thinking about.
4. Career pivot — "I want to pivot my career"
   Approach: Prove you belong in the direction you're moving toward — not the one you're leaving.
   First step: One company. Pick one in the direction you're moving toward — not the one you're leaving.

What an analysis adds: upload your LinkedIn data export at https://rankedin.app/dashboard/upload?source=claude. RankedIn then checks your own export for signals that point to a path — recent job applications or saved jobs, self-employment in your current role, and elevated AI risk for your role — and adds your profile scores, peer comparison and AI resilience. Each path also has a guided, step-by-step version on rankedin.app once your analysis is complete.
